# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the DiffPal community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project. Acceptable
behavior includes giving actionable feedback, acknowledging mistakes, and
showing empathy. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized
attention, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
